# Turn 1, priced option by option: `R32 SWA from own`

500 greedy self-play games from the network's own; 20,112 turn-1 decisions, each option played over ~96 worlds to the end of the turn and valued by the critic there. Gains are in points of the decider's score (a whole game is 100); ± is one standard error.

* **lead of the priced best**: its mean lead over the network's move on all worlds -- an upper bound, since the best of several noisy options is picked on the same worlds it is measured on;
* **honest gain**: the best option chosen on half the worlds, measured on the other half -- what switching to the priced best is expected to be worth; where the options are close it can fall below zero (the half picks a worse option), so it is a lower bound on what a perfect pick would gain;
* **clear**: the priced best ahead of the network's move by 3+ SE over all worlds.
