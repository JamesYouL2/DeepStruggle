A lead counts only if its conservative bound (lead − 2 SE) reaches this. In a game that is won or lost anyway, every move is worth about the same.

Positions open here with ?pos=. The workbench must run this engine's save format.

Keys: j/k next/previous, o open, 1/2/3 mark that move good → bad → unmarked, 4 unclear, c clear, l leaning, b bank.

Value: each move searched (64 simulations) in each of several deals of the hidden cards (bank_clarity.py --worlds, 4 by default), as the mover's expected result from −1 to +1, ± its standard error. Lead: the best move's advantage over the next, in win-probability points; sure subtracts two standard errors. Value gap order: largest lead first, halved when playouts show the two moves within 3 points of each other even at two standard errors. Playouts: the network plays each move out to the end, same dice and redeal per pair; the share of games the mover wins.
